# Surface: / (the profile wall)

Mode: Operate. The visitor completes a task: write, share, see it land.
Audience and job: reviewers checking that they can share a message, that it is saved, and that the feed is newest-first after a refresh, on desktop or phone.
Scene: a reviewer at a desk in daytime office light, in a browser tab. Light scene, like the product it echoes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a social-network profile page in the shape of today's facebook.com, owned by Raven, where the composer is the hero. It refuses the category default of a bare textarea over a bulleted list.

OWN-WORLD: cool grey canvas #F0F2F5, white cards (8 px radius, 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.2) shadow), one blue #0866FF for primary actions and links, ink #050505, secondary #65676B, dividers #CED0D4. System UI stack (Segoe UI, Helvetica, Arial). Circular avatars. A white top bar with our own "wall" wordmark in blue. No Facebook logo or name.

STORY: the visitor sees whose wall this is (cover, avatar, name, intro), writes in "Write something to Raven…", optionally adds a photo, presses Share, and watches the post appear at the top of the feed, live for every open tab.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- **Top:** a 56 px top bar.
- **Profile header:** a cover image about 300 px high, a 168 px circular avatar overlapping it bottom-left, the name in 32 px bold, a one-line role, and a tab row (Posts active).
- **Desktop:** two columns below the header, Intro on the left (360 px) and the composer and feed on the right (500 px).
- **Phone:** one column, composer first.
- **Share:** the primary blue button at the composer's bottom right.

FORM: pinned by the user ("make it similar to facebook.com"). seed: none (pinned) "for the look, utilize impecabble skill, we'll create a new one, but make it similar to facebook.com".
Signature interaction: a shared post slides into the top of the feed with a brief blue highlight that fades, and posts from other tabs arrive the same way with a "New" marker. Motion grammar: the new-post arrival only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
